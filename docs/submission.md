## Description (draft, about 120 words)

Chaos is a multiplayer historical role-play that runs in the browser — no sign-up, no install, phones and
laptops. Rome, 15 March 44 BC: you and your friends become Caesar, Brutus, Cassius, Calpurnia or Mark
Antony, each with a secret and a goal. Type in plain English; an AI scribe rewrites every line in the voice
of the era. Every role nobody picked is played by an AI character with a hidden personality, so you can
play alone — and a friend who arrives late can take over any AI character mid-game. History moves on a
10-minute clock; at key moments one character must decide, and the ending — one of six — compares History
with your timeline. Stuck for words? Press Tab for in-character ideas, or just speak.
