## Cover image — description / prompt

> A slab of pale veined marble lit softly from the left, with "CHAOS" carved into it in classical Roman
> capitals, and below it, smaller, "THE IDES OF MARCH". In the top-right corner a small square blood-red
> seal stamp. Behind the slab, out of focus, the steps and columns of a Roman senate house at dawn, and an
> unrolled scroll on the steps. Colors: porphyry purple shadows, aged bronze highlights, marble white, dark
> stone ink, one blood-red accent. Calm, dramatic, painterly; no people's faces, no blood, no weapons.

Image generators often misspell lettering: if the title comes out wrong, generate the scene without text
and add the title afterwards (font: Cinzel, as in the game).
